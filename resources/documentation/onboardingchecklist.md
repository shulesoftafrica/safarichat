# SafariChat — New Client Onboarding Checklist

> **Purpose**: Follow these steps in order. Each section must be completed before moving to the next. Expected total setup time: **20–30 minutes**.

---

## PHASE 1 — Create Your Account
**URL**: `https://safarichat.africa/register`

### Step 1.1 — Choose Your Business Type
- [ ] Open the registration page and look for the **"What type of business do you run?"** screen.
- [ ] Click on the card that best describes your business:
  - 🏪 Retail & Shop
  - 💼 Services
  - 🍽️ Food & Restaurant
  - 🚚 Delivery & Logistics
  - 🏥 Healthcare
  - 📚 Education & Training
  - 🏠 Real Estate
  - 🏢 Other Business
- [ ] Click **Next** to proceed to Step 2.

---

### Step 1.2 — Fill in Your Business Profile
- [ ] Enter your **Business Name** (the name your customers know you by).
- [ ] Enter the **Owner / Manager Name** (your full name).
- [ ] Select **Business Size**:
  - Just me (Solo entrepreneur)
  - Small team (2–10 people)
  - Medium business (11–50 people)
  - Large business (50+ people)
- [ ] Select your **Country** (detected automatically from your phone number).
- [ ] Select your **City / Region**.
- [ ] Select **Years in Business** (optional but recommended for AI personalization).
- [ ] Check all boxes that apply under **"How do you currently communicate with customers?"** (phone calls, WhatsApp, email, walk-ins, etc.).
- [ ] Check all boxes under **"What are your main business goals?"** (increase sales, reduce response time, manage leads, etc.).
- [ ] Click **Next** to proceed to Step 3.

---

### Step 1.3 — Verify Your Phone Number (OTP)
- [ ] Enter your **WhatsApp phone number** with country code (e.g., +255712345678).
- [ ] Click **Send OTP**.
- [ ] Check your WhatsApp — you will receive a 6-digit verification code.
- [ ] Enter the code in the verification field.
- [ ] Click **Verify & Create Account**.
- [ ] You will be redirected automatically to the WhatsApp connection page.

> **Note**: The OTP is sent via WhatsApp. Make sure the phone number you enter is an active WhatsApp number you can access right now.

---

## PHASE 2 — Connect Your WhatsApp Business Number
**URL**: `https://safarichat.africa/whatsapp/connect`  
*(You are redirected here automatically after registration)*

### Step 2.1 — Enter Your WhatsApp Business Phone Number
- [ ] On the **"Connect Your WhatsApp"** page, look for the phone number input field.
- [ ] Enter the WhatsApp Business number you want to use for your business (this can be different from the number you registered with).
- [ ] Make sure your phone has WhatsApp installed and is connected to the internet.
- [ ] Click **Generate QR Code**.

---

### Step 2.2 — Scan the QR Code
- [ ] Open **WhatsApp** on your phone.
- [ ] Go to **Settings** → **Linked Devices** → **Link a Device**.
- [ ] Point your phone camera at the QR Code shown on the screen.
- [ ] Wait 5–15 seconds — the system will automatically detect the successful connection.
- [ ] You will see the message **"WhatsApp Connected Successfully!"** on screen.

> **If the QR code expires**: Click the refresh button to generate a new QR code and try again.

---

### Step 2.3 — Confirm and Continue
- [ ] Confirm your phone number is shown correctly in the success screen.
- [ ] Click **Set Up Products/Services** to move to the next phase.

> **Your WhatsApp is now live.** Any message sent to this number will appear in SafariChat.

---

## PHASE 3 — Add Your Products or Services
**URL**: `https://safarichat.africa/products`  
*(Or navigate from the menu: Products → Add Product)*

### Step 3.1 — Add Your First Product or Service
- [ ] Click **Add Product** (top-right button on the Products page).
- [ ] Fill in the **Product Name** (e.g., "Office Chair", "Hair Braiding Service", "Lunch Delivery").
- [ ] Write a **Description** — be detailed. The AI will use this to answer customer questions.
- [ ] Set the **Price** and select your **Currency**.
- [ ] (Optional) Add a **Discount** or promotional price.
- [ ] (Optional) Set **Stock / Inventory** level if you sell physical goods.
- [ ] (Optional) Upload one or more **Product Images**.
- [ ] (Optional) Attach a **PDF or document** (spec sheet, brochure, menu, etc.).
- [ ] Toggle **"Enable AI Recommendations"** to ON — this lets the AI suggest this product in conversations.
- [ ] Click **Save Product**.

### Step 3.2 — Add All Remaining Products/Services
- [ ] Repeat Step 3.1 for every product or service you sell.
- [ ] Aim to add at least **3–5 products** before activating the AI agent.

> **Tip**: The more product detail you add (description, price, FAQs), the smarter and more accurate the AI's responses will be.

---

## PHASE 4 — Configure Your AI Sales Agent
**URL**: `https://safarichat.africa/ai-agents/create`  
*(Or navigate: AI Agents → Create New Agent)*

### Step 4.1 — Create the AI Agent
- [ ] Click **Create New Agent**.
- [ ] Give the agent a **Name** (e.g., "Amina", "SalesBot", "Support Agent").
- [ ] Select the **WhatsApp Instance** this agent should be linked to (the number you connected in Phase 2).
- [ ] Select the **Purpose** of this agent:
  - Sales Inquiries
  - Customer Support
  - General Business
  - Marketing
  - Order Processing
- [ ] Write a **Greeting Message** — this is the first thing the AI says when a new customer messages you (e.g., *"Hello! Welcome to [Business Name]. How can I help you today?"*).

---

### Step 4.2 — Set AI Behaviour Rules
- [ ] Set **AI Personality Style**:
  - Professional (formal, business language)
  - Friendly (warm and conversational)
  - Casual (relaxed tone)
- [ ] Configure **Escalation Rules** — define when the AI should hand over to a human agent:
  - After X failed attempts to answer a question
  - When the customer sends a negative message/complaint
  - When price negotiation goes beyond a discount threshold
- [ ] (Optional) Set **Business Hours** — the AI can inform customers when your team is offline.
- [ ] (Optional) Read and accept the **AI Agent Terms and Conditions** (link shown on the page).

---

### Step 4.3 — Activate the Agent
- [ ] Review all settings.
- [ ] Toggle the agent **Status** to **Active**.
- [ ] Click **Save**.

> **Test it**: Send a WhatsApp message to your connected number and watch the AI respond within seconds.

---

## PHASE 5 — Import or Add Your Contacts
**URL**: `https://safarichat.africa/guest`  
*(Navigate: Contacts / Customers from the left menu)*

### Step 5.1 — Add Contacts (choose one method)

**Option A — Manual Entry**
- [ ] Click **Add Contact**.
- [ ] Enter Name, Phone Number, Email (optional), and Location.
- [ ] Assign a **Lead Stage**: New / Contacted / Qualified / Converted.
- [ ] Click **Save**.

**Option B — Import from CSV File**
- [ ] Click **Upload / Import Contacts**.
- [ ] Download the CSV template (if provided) and fill it with your contact list.
- [ ] Upload the completed CSV file.
- [ ] Review the import preview and confirm.

**Option C — Sync from Your WhatsApp**
- [ ] Click **Sync WhatsApp Contacts**.
- [ ] The system will pull contacts from your connected WhatsApp number automatically.
- [ ] Review and confirm which contacts to import.

---

### Step 5.2 — Organise Your Contacts
- [ ] Assign **Priority Levels** (High, Medium, Low) to important leads.
- [ ] Review and update **Lead Stage** for each contact:
  - New — just arrived
  - Contacted — you have messaged them
  - Qualified — confirmed interest in buying
  - Converted — completed a purchase
- [ ] Add **tags or notes** to contacts as needed.

---

## PHASE 6 — Send Your First Campaign (Optional but Recommended)
**URL**: `https://safarichat.africa/campaigns/create`  
*(Navigate: Campaigns → Create Campaign)*

### Step 6.1 — Create a Campaign
- [ ] Click **Create New Campaign**.
- [ ] Enter a **Campaign Name** (e.g., "April Launch Offer", "Welcome Message to New Leads").
- [ ] Write your **Message**. You can:
  - Use text, images, or documents
  - Use personalization variables like `{{name}}` to insert the customer's name
- [ ] Select **Recipients**:
  - All Contacts
  - Contacts by Lead Stage (e.g., only "New" leads)
  - Custom list of specific phone numbers

---

### Step 6.2 — Schedule or Send
- [ ] Choose **Send Now** to broadcast immediately, or
- [ ] Choose **Schedule** and select a date and time for delivery.
- [ ] Click **Launch Campaign**.
- [ ] Monitor delivery on the **Campaign Report** page (Campaigns → Reports).

> **Important**: Start with a small test group first. New WhatsApp numbers warm up gradually — send to a maximum of 20–50 contacts on your first day to avoid spam flags.

---

## PHASE 7 — Complete Your Business Settings
**URL**: `https://safarichat.africa/settings`  
*(Navigate: Settings from the top-right profile menu)*

### Step 7.1 — Business Profile
- [ ] Upload your **Business Logo**.
- [ ] Confirm your **Business Name** and **Contact Information** are correct.
- [ ] Add your **Business Address** (helps the AI answer location queries).
- [ ] Add your **Business Hours** (opening and closing times).

### Step 7.2 — Notifications
- [ ] Configure **Email Notifications** for new leads and conversation escalations.
- [ ] Enable **WhatsApp Alerts** to receive important system alerts on your phone.

### Step 7.3 — Billing & Subscription
- [ ] Navigate to **Billing** (Settings → Billing or Transactions).
- [ ] Review your current **subscription plan** and message limits.
- [ ] Add a **payment method** if required.
- [ ] Ensure your account is active to avoid service interruption.

### Step 7.4 — Team Members (Optional)
- [ ] Navigate to the **Team** section.
- [ ] Click **Add Team Member**.
- [ ] Enter the team member's name, email, and role.
- [ ] Assign them to the relevant WhatsApp line or function (Sales, Support, etc.).
- [ ] They will receive an invitation to join.

---

## PHASE 8 — Verify Everything is Working
**URL**: `https://safarichat.africa/home` (Dashboard)

### Final Verification Checklist
- [ ] **WhatsApp Status**: Go to **WhatsApp → Status**. Confirm your number shows as **Connected / Ready** (green indicator).
- [ ] **AI Agent Active**: Go to **AI Agents** and confirm your agent shows **Active**.
- [ ] **Products Added**: Go to **Products** and confirm your items are listed.
- [ ] **Contacts Present**: Go to **Contacts** and confirm your contacts are imported.
- [ ] **Test a Conversation**: Send a WhatsApp message to your business number from a different phone. Confirm the AI responds.
- [ ] **Dashboard Metrics**: Check the **Dashboard** — message counts, lead counts, and connection status should all reflect recent activity.

## Troubleshooting Common Issues

**QR Code expired before I could scan it**
- Click the refresh icon on the QR Code page to generate a new one. You have approximately 60 seconds to scan each code.

**WhatsApp says "Linked Devices limit reached"**
- Open WhatsApp on your phone → Settings → Linked Devices. Remove any old or unused device, then try scanning again.

**AI is not responding to customer messages**
- Check that your AI Agent status is **Active** under AI Agents.
- Check that your WhatsApp instance is **Connected** under WhatsApp Status.
- Ensure your subscription plan has remaining message credits.

**OTP code never arrived**
- Make sure the phone number you entered is a WhatsApp-active number.
- Wait 60 seconds and try requesting the OTP again.
- If still not received, contact support.

**Contacts not appearing after CSV import**
- Ensure phone numbers in the CSV include the country code (e.g., +255712345678).
- Make sure the CSV file uses the correct column headers (download the provided template).
