## 🚀 Getting Started: Quick Setup Guide

### Step 1: Account Registration (5 minutes)
1. Visit SafariChat platform and click "Register"
2. Enter business name, email, phone number, and location
3. Verify phone number via OTP (one-time password)
4. Choose your subscription plan
5. Complete payment setup

### Step 2: WhatsApp Connection (2 minutes)
1. Navigate to WhatsApp Setup page
2. Click "Connect WhatsApp"
3. Scan QR code with your WhatsApp Business phone
4. Wait for automatic connection confirmation
5. Set instance name and purpose (e.g., "Sales Line")
6.  Done! Your WhatsApp is now connected

### Step 3: Product Setup (10 minutes)
1. Go to Products section
2. Click "Add Product"
3. Enter product name, description, price
4. Upload product images and documents
5. Enable AI product recommendations
6. Save and repeat for all products

### Step 4: AI Configuration (5 minutes)
1. Navigate to AI Settings
2. Choose AI personality (Professional, Friendly, Casual)
3. Set AI greeting message
4. Configure escalation rules (when to transfer to human)
5. Test AI responses with sample questions
6. Activate AI assistant

### Step 5: Team Setup (Optional, 5 minutes)
1. Go to Team Management
2. Click "Add Team Member"
3. Enter name, email, role
4. Assign to specific WhatsApp lines or functions
5. Team member receives invitation email

### Total Setup Time: 20-30 minutes to full operation

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### General Questions

**Q: What is SafariChat?**  
A: SafariChat is an AI-powered WhatsApp business automation platform that helps businesses manage customer conversations, automate responses, qualify leads, and increase sales through intelligent automation.

**Q: How is SafariChat different from regular WhatsApp Business?**  
A: Regular WhatsApp Business is limited to manual responses and basic automation. SafariChat adds AI-powered conversations, advanced lead management, campaign broadcasting, product catalog management, team collaboration, analytics, and much more.

**Q: Do I need technical skills to use SafariChat?**  
A: No technical skills required. The platform is designed for business owners and teams. Setup takes 20-30 minutes with our step-by-step guides.

**Q: Can I use SafariChat with my existing WhatsApp Business number?**  
A: Yes! You can connect your existing WhatsApp Business account using QR code scanning.

**Q: How many WhatsApp numbers can I connect?**  
A: Depends on your plan. Starter plan: 1 line, Professional: 3 lines, Enterprise: unlimited lines.

### AI & Automation

**Q: How accurate is the AI?**  
A: Our AI successfully handles 80-90% of routine inquiries without human intervention. For complex questions, it seamlessly escalates to human agents.

**Q: Can I customize what the AI says?**  
A: Yes! You can configure AI personality, greeting messages, and escalation rules. You can also provide FAQs and product information for AI to reference.

**Q: Will the AI work in my language?**  
A: The AI supports 50+ languages and automatically detects and responds in the customer's language.

**Q: What happens when AI can't answer?**  
A: The AI recognizes its limitations and smoothly transfers the conversation to a human agent with full context of the conversation.

### Pricing & Billing

**Q: What is included in the free trial?**  
A: 30-day free trial with full access to all features (usage limits apply). No credit card required to start.

**Q: Can I change plans later?**  
A: Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade anytime from your dashboard. Changes take effect immediately.

**Q: What are AI credits?**  
A: AI credits represent AI conversation capacity. Each AI response consumes credits. Plans include monthly credit allocations.

## 📖 Glossary of Terms

**AI Credits**: Units representing AI processing capacity, consumed when AI generates responses

**Campaign**: Broadcast message sent to multiple contacts simultaneously

**Conversion Rate**: Percentage of leads that become paying customers

**Escalation**: Transfer of conversation from AI to human agent

**Instance**: Individual WhatsApp Business line connected to SafariChat

**Lead**: Potential customer captured in the system

**Lead Score**: AI-calculated value representing likelihood of conversion

**Nurture Sequence**: Series of automated follow-up messages over time

**QR Code**: Quick Response code used to connect WhatsApp to SafariChat

**RAG**: Retrieval-Augmented Generation - AI technique for accurate product information

**Webhook**: Automated notification sent to external systems when events occur

**WhatsApp Line**: Individual WhatsApp Business phone number connected to platform
